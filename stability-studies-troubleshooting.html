Stability Studies and Analytical Troubleshooting

Analytical support for investigating changes over time, unexpected results and challenging laboratory problems during pharmaceutical development and quality-control work.

Discuss a problemHPLC method services

Investigate results, understand trends and identify next steps

Unexpected analytical results can arise from product changes, sample handling, method performance, instrument conditions or interactions between these factors. Phanso provides focused analytical investigation to help identify plausible causes and determine what to examine next.

Stability-related work can involve analysis of samples from agreed storage conditions and assessment of analytical trends. The study design, storage conditions, test methods, time points and reporting requirements should be defined for each project.

Areas of support

  • Analytical testing of stability samples within an agreed scope
  • Investigation of assay changes and degradation-related peaks
  • HPLC/UPLC troubleshooting, including peak shape and resolution
  • Investigation of sample preparation, recovery and reproducibility
  • Review of analytical trends and unexpected results
  • Follow-up method optimization where required

Clear scope and interpretation

Stability testing requirements depend on the product, intended purpose and applicable regulatory framework. Phanso can discuss the proposed analytical work and reporting scope before a project begins.

Formal stability programmes, shelf-life claims, GMP testing or regulatory submissions should only be represented as such where the required facilities, procedures, quality system and study design are in place.

Examples of problems to investigate

Focused troubleshooting can help distinguish a product-related change from an analytical or sample-handling issue.

Unexpected assay results

Investigate changes in analytical response, recovery or repeatability.

New or changing peaks

Assess chromatographic changes and develop suitable follow-up investigations.

Chromatographic performance

Investigate retention, resolution, peak shape, baseline behaviour or pressure changes.

Stability trends

Evaluate analytical results over time within the agreed study design and test scope.

Need help understanding an unexpected result?

Share the product, method, observed change and any relevant study conditions.
