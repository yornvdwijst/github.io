About Phanso

Independent expertise. Practical analytical thinking.

Phanso B.V. (Pharmaceutical Analytical Solutions) is an independent laboratory in the Netherlands focused on pharmaceutical R&D, with particular attention to veterinary medicinal products.

We combine product-focused investigation with analytical method development to help clients understand formulation behaviour, generate useful analytical data and investigate difficult technical questions.

Our work can support projects at different stages, from early formulation research and analytical characterization to method optimization and quality-control support.

How Phanso can help

  • Develop or optimize an HPLC/UPLC method
  • Investigate an unexpected analytical result
  • Characterize or compare pharmaceutical formulations
  • Assess stability-related analytical changes
  • Support pharmaceutical R&D and QC challenges
