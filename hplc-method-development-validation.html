Analytical methods designed around the question

A useful analytical method needs to be appropriate for the product, analyte and intended purpose. Phanso develops and optimizes HPLC and UPLC methods to address practical analytical requirements and investigate difficult chromatographic behaviour.

Depending on the project, method development may involve selection of chromatographic conditions, optimization of retention and resolution, evaluation of peak shape, sample preparation, detection wavelength and robustness-related parameters.

Analytical services

  • HPLC and UPLC method development and optimization
  • Assay and content determination
  • Impurity and degradation-product analysis
  • Stability-indicating method development
  • Investigation of peak shape, resolution, pressure or reproducibility issues
  • Analytical method validation or verification, where agreed

Validation with a defined scope

Validation activities should be based on the method's intended use and applicable requirements. The scope and performance characteristics to be assessed can be agreed before the study starts.

Phanso does not imply that every project is conducted under GMP or that a method is automatically suitable for regulated release testing. Applicable quality requirements should be discussed for each engagement.

Common analytical challenges

Phanso can help investigate problems that affect method performance or interpretation of results.

Retention and resolution

Optimize chromatographic conditions when compounds are poorly retained or insufficiently separated.

Peak shape and pressure

Investigate tailing, broadening, rising backpressure and other chromatographic symptoms.

Assay and recovery

Review sample preparation, recovery and analytical response when results are unexpected.

Stability-indicating analysis

Develop methods to help assess the active ingredient alongside relevant degradation-related changes.
